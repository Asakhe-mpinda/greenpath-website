Growing Greener Communities, Together

GreenPath Community Foundation empowers local communities across Gauteng to protect and restore their environment through education, hands-on action, and lasting partnerships.

Become a Volunteer

Who We Are

Since our founding, GreenPath has run tree-planting drives, recycling education workshops, and community clean-up campaigns in partnership with local schools and municipalities. We believe every community has the power to shape a more sustainable future.

Our Community Impact

GreenPath focuses on practical environmental action that communities can continue after each project. Our work encourages people to care for shared spaces, make responsible waste choices, and take part in activities that improve neighbourhoods for future generations.

  • Greener spaces: Supporting tree planting and the restoration of community areas.
  • Less waste: Promoting recycling, reuse, and responsible waste disposal.
  • Community action: Connecting residents, schools, businesses, and volunteers around local environmental goals.

What We Do

  • Tree Planting

  • Recycling Education
