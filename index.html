Growing Greener Communities, Together

GreenPath Community Foundation empowers local communities across Gauteng to protect and restore their environment through education, hands-on action, and lasting partnerships.

Become a Volunteer

Who We Are

Since our founding, GreenPath has run tree-planting drives, recycling education workshops, and community clean-up campaigns in partnership with local schools and municipalities. We believe every community has the power to shape a more sustainable future.

What We Do

  • Tree Planting

    Restoring green spaces one neighbourhood at a time.

  • Recycling Education

    Teaching households and schools how to reduce waste.

  • Community Clean-Ups

    Bringing volunteers together to clean up local parks and rivers.
