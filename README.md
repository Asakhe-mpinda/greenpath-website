# greenpath-website

The GreenPath Community Foundation website is a five-page informational and lead-generation site built for a hypothetical environmental non-profit organisation operating across Gauteng. The site's purpose is to raise awareness of GreenPath's mission, showcase its three core programmes (tree planting, recycling education, and community clean-ups), and convert visitors into volunteers or sponsors through a dedicated enquiry form.

The homepage introduces the organisation with a hero section and calls to action; the About Us page details GreenPath's history, mission, vision, and team; the Programmes page describes each initiative in depth; the Get Involved page hosts a form allowing visitors to register interest as either a volunteer or a sponsor/partner; and the Contact page lists two physical office locations (Johannesburg and Soweto) with embedded maps and a general contact form.

The site is built with semantic, standards-compliant HTML5 (Part 1), with responsive CSS styling and JavaScript-driven interactivity to follow in Parts 2 and 3. It is structured for accessibility and mobile readiness, with consistent navigation and footer links across every page.
